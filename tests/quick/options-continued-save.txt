# A battle saved in mid-battle on a save with Extra Options of its own, for options-continued-load.txt (a tester on
# 0.9.39, 2026-11-25: a battle continued from its autosave ran on the mod's settings, not the save's: a party member
# turned to a crystal for good with Player Deaths on "Revive after battle", and JP came at 125% for the save's 200%).
# Run with
#   python -I tools/quick_test.py --restart --stop tests/quick/options-continued-save.txt
# On the world map the save is given options the mod's settings don't have: JP and EXP 200%, spillover 1/2 (var
# 0x14 with its marker) and "Revive after battle" (flag 0xDD). Then 1-A's last battle as in dorter-resume-save.txt:
# the party's first turn waits, and the game's autosave at its next turn carries the variables.
name A battle saved on a save with its own Extra Options
send testmode record
wait world depth=1 state=0 timeout=30
wait seconds 2
setvar 0x14 0x1002C8C8
setvar 0xDD 1
wait log "options: the save's: player deaths ReviveAfterBattle, .* JP 200%" timeout=10
scenario 0x34B noted
wait log "formation: screen open" timeout=120
formation units=1
wait log "scenario 0x34B's first walk, battle-start flag 1" press=RETURN every=2 timeout=120
wait unit player=1 present=1 turn=1 timeout=180
key DOWN wait=1.5
key DOWN wait=1.5
key RETURN wait=1.5
wait seconds 3
key RETURN wait=1.5
wait seconds 20
wait unit player=1 present=1 turn=1 timeout=300
wait seconds 5
expect var 0x14 0x1002C8C8
expect var 0xDD 1
