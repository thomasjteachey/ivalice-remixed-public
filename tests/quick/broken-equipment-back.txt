# Broken equipment comes back after the battle (a player's request on 0.9.40, 2026-11-27: the Rendezvous knights break
# gear constantly). With the mod's Broken equipment setting at BackAfterBattle, an item broken on one of your units goes
# back in the inventory as the battle ends, as a stolen one does. Run with
#   python -I tools/quick_test.py --restart --location 24 --stop tests/quick/broken-equipment-back.txt
# A random battle on Mandalia Plains, the setting on for this run (`send option`, Config.json untouched), two units
# placed, each given Arts of War (0x07) as its secondary with Rend Helm (0x8A: bit 0x40 of +0xA8, the Knight's learned
# bits), PA 99 and a Grand Helmet (0x9C) on its head (+0x1A), none in the inventory. The first of them to act uses
# Rend Helm on the other (`ally`); the break is noted, the enemies are downed, and after the battle the inventory holds
# one Grand Helmet.
name Broken equipment comes back after the battle
send testmode record
send option BrokenEquipment BackAfterBattle
wait world depth=1 state=0 timeout=20
key RETURN
wait log "reveal treasures: map" press=RETURN every=3 timeout=180
formation units=2
poke unit player=1 present=1 offset=0x13 hex=07
poke unit player=1 present=1 offset=0xA8 or=40
poke unit player=1 present=1 offset=0x3E hex=63
poke unit player=1 present=1 offset=0x1A hex=9C00
send poke +0x11A7C9C 00
send act 07 08A ally
wait log "harness: unit \d+ uses 0x08A" timeout=240
wait log "taken equipment: item 0x09C broken on unit \d+ comes back after the battle" timeout=60
shot broken
defeat
wait log "taken equipment: 1 of item 0x09C back in the inventory" press=RETURN every=3 timeout=240
expect peek +0x11A7C9C 01
