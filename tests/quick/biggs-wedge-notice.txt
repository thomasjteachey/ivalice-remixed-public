# Biggs and Wedge's scene at Gariland (ReMixed's 0x0A4, the mod's 0x2FF = event 767) to its end with Auto-proceed on
# and no presses. Its last line is the notice "Rendezvous missions are now posted among the Rumors in taverns." (message
# 19); a player saw the game sit on it with Auto-proceed on (reported 2026-11-17). With TIMED_NOTICES the notice closes
# on its own after ReMixed's 400 frames, so the scene ends and sets flag 0x92 without a key pressed. Run with
# --restart --location 6 (Gariland). The scene takes about four minutes on Auto-proceed.
name Biggs and Wedge Rendezvous notice
wait world depth=1 state=0 timeout=20
setvar 0x92 0
scenario 0x2FF
wait log "loaded event 767" timeout=60
wait seconds 4
key SHIFT
wait seconds 2
shot auto-proceed-on
wait event none timeout=330
expect var 0x92 1
wait seconds 3
shot world
